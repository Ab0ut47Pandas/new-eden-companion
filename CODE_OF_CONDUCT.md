# Community rules

Keep discussions about the project and the submitted change.

- Be direct without being abusive.
- Do not post another player's private information.
- Do not submit stolen fits, documentation, or artwork.
- Do not use issues or pull requests for recruitment, politics, scams, or RMT.
- Back up factual corrections with an EVE client check, CCP documentation, or reproducible data when possible.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove material that breaks these rules and may block repeat offenders.
