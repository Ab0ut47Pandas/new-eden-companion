# New Eden Companion — Good Habits Requirements

Good-habits coaching is mandatory before `REL-01`. Mining is the preferred first complete vertical slice because it can teach preparation, attention, replacement risk, and escape/recovery without pretending NEC sees live client state.

## Coaching dimensions

When relevant to an activity, NEC should explain:

- performance versus survivability tradeoffs;
- awareness expectations and what the player must watch manually;
- escape and recovery planning;
- replacement risk and whether the activity assumes losses are affordable;
- solo versus fleet/support assumptions;
- attention requirements and the danger of treating an activity as more passive than it is.

The UI should prioritize the few habits that matter for the current activity instead of dumping every possible warning on the player.

## Mining vertical slice

The first good-habits slice should connect mining readiness with an evidence-backed ship/fit, resource goal, cargo/hauling considerations, replacement exposure, awareness, escape/recovery preparation, and the reason the mined resource matters to the active goal where known.

NEC must not claim that a route, belt, anomaly, fit, or location is safe; must not claim live awareness of threats; and must preserve unknown location/risk information explicitly.

## Manual checkpoint

Before `REL-01`, the installed mining flow must be manually tested for whether the coaching is useful without becoming warning spam, and whether the distinction between performance, survivability, attention, and replacement risk is understandable.
